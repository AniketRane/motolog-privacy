MotoLog Privacy Policy

Effective 18 October 2026

MotoLog is an offline vehicle logbook for Android. This policy explains what happens to the information you enter in the app. In short: everything stays on your device. MotoLog has no account, no server, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

Information you enter

You can record vehicles, fuel fill-ups, odometer readings, services, expenses, reminders and documents, and attach files such as receipts or insurance papers. This information is stored only in MotoLog's private storage on your device. The developer never receives it, cannot see it and has no copy of it.

Information we collect

None. MotoLog makes no network requests. It contains no analytics, crash reporting, advertising or third-party tracking SDKs, and it does not use the Android advertising ID.

Sharing

MotoLog does not share your information with anyone. Data leaves your device only when you choose to export a backup or CSV file, or share an attachment, and it goes only to the app or location you pick in the Android share sheet or file picker. What happens to it after that is governed by the app or service you chose.

Permissions

  • Notifications: requested only when you turn on reminders, so MotoLog can alert you before a service, renewal or reminder is due. Notifications are scheduled locally on your device.
  • Files: attachments are chosen with the Android system file picker. MotoLog does not request access to all of your files or media.
  • Internet: Android's WebView needs this permission to load the app's own bundled files. MotoLog does not use it to contact any server.

Storage, security and deletion

Your records live in the app's private storage, protected by Android's app sandbox and your device's encryption. MotoLog does not add its own encryption, and Android cloud backup is turned off for MotoLog. You can delete individual records, delete a vehicle with all its records, or erase everything with Settings › Backup, restore & export › Reset MotoLog. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage permanently deletes everything, so export a backup first if you want to keep your data.

Children

MotoLog is intended for vehicle owners and is not directed at children under 13.
